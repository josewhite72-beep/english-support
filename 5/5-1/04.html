Ábrelo solo después de responder.

Carla: Hi, Luis! What do you like doing after school?

Luis: I like running in the park.

Carla: Do you like swimming?

Luis: Yes, I do! I like swimming on Sundays.

Carla: Me too! And do you like dancing?

Luis: No, I don't. But my sister likes dancing a lot.

Carla: When do you run?

Luis: In the afternoon, at five o'clock.

Carla: Can I run with you tomorrow?

Luis: Sure!
