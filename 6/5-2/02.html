Marco: Grandpa, how long has our school been here?

Grandpa: It has been here since 1965. That is a long time!

Marco: Were you a student there?

Grandpa: Yes! I was a student there for six years.

Marco: Has the building changed?

Grandpa: Yes, it has. In 1965 there were only three classrooms. Now there are twelve classrooms.

Marco: Has the school had many principals?

Grandpa: Yes, it has. It has had eight principals since 1965.

Marco: Do you remember your teacher?

Grandpa: Of course! Miss Rosa. She was my teacher for two years.

Marco: Our school has a long history!
