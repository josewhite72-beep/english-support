Ábrelo solo después de responder.

Ana: Diego, look at the sky! The clouds are very dark.

Diego: Oh no! It's going to rain.

Ana: But we have soccer practice this afternoon.

Diego: Let's check the forecast on my phone. It says it will rain from three to five o'clock.

Ana: Will it rain tomorrow?

Diego: No, it won't. Tomorrow will be sunny, but very hot. Thirty-three degrees!

Ana: Then we should play tomorrow morning, before it gets hot.

Diego: Good idea. I'm going to tell the coach.
