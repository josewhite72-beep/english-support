Tomás: Hi, Sofía! I'm new here. What is there in this community?

Sofía: Hi, Tomás! There is a market, a park, a church, and a school.

Tomás: Which place is the busiest?

Sofía: The market. It's busier than the park, especially in the morning.

Tomás: And which building is the oldest?

Sofía: The church. It's older than the school. The school is the newest building.

Tomás: Is the park big?

Sofía: Yes! It's bigger than the market, and it's the most beautiful place, with big trees.

Tomás: Cool! Where can I play soccer?

Sofía: In the park, in the afternoon. It's quieter then.
