Carmen: Good morning, Miss Rosa. Can I ask you some questions for the school newspaper?

Miss Rosa: Of course, Carmen!

Carmen: How long have you been a teacher?

Miss Rosa: I have been a teacher for twenty-five years.

Carmen: And how long have you worked at this school?

Miss Rosa: I have worked here since 2010.

Carmen: Has the school changed?

Miss Rosa: Yes, it has. We have had a computer room since 2018, and the library is new.

Carmen: What is your favorite place in the school?

Miss Rosa: The garden. The students have planted many trees.

Carmen: Thank you, Miss Rosa!
