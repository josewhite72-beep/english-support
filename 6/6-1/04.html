Ábrelo solo después de responder.

Diego: Ana, let's go to the river on Saturday!

Ana: Good idea! But what will the weather be like?

Diego: The forecast says it will be sunny in the morning.

Ana: And in the afternoon?

Diego: It will be cloudy, and later it will be rainy.

Ana: So we should go early. What time?

Diego: At eight o'clock. It will be hot, about thirty degrees.

Ana: Then we should bring water and a hat.

Diego: And an umbrella, for the rain later!

Ana: OK. See you on Saturday!
